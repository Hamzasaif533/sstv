How to receive

  1. Open the Receive page on another device, or an SSTV app: Robot36 (Android), CQ SSTV (iPhone), MMSSTV (Windows), QSSTV (Linux).
  2. Hold it near this computer's speaker, or feed the audio into a radio.
  3. Press Transmit. The receiver reads the mode from the header and draws the picture line by line.

On the air, make sure you're licensed and on an SSTV frequency (e.g. 14.230 MHz USB).
